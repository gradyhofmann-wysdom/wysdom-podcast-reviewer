  1. Press play and listen. Use the 10 buttons to jump back or forward ten seconds, and the 1× button to speed things up.
  2. Hear something worth flagging? Tap the red mic button at the far right of the controls. The episode pauses automatically so you can talk.
  3. Tap the red button again to save — while you're recording it pulses and turns into a stop square. Your note is pinned to that exact second, so there are no timestamps to write down.
  4. Keep going. Everything saves as you go, so you can close the page and pick it back up later.
  5. When you're done, scroll to the bottom if you'd like to record a closing commentary for the episode.
